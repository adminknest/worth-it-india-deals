# Worth It India organic growth plan

## Goal

Reach 1,000 genuine Telegram subscribers as a 30-day stretch goal. Use 600 retained subscribers as the operating target and 250 as the minimum proof that distribution is working. The business metric is retained buyers and confirmed commission, not raw joins.

Worth It India's promise is: **shopping deals that are genuinely worth the current price**. The catalogue may span categories, but every post must show a current price, reference price, verification time, merchant, material coupon or variant conditions, and affiliate disclosure.

## Publishing product

- Check and score candidates continuously.
- Publish 12–20 verified deals between 9 a.m. and 11 p.m. IST, with no more than two or three ordinary posts per hour.
- Add an 8:30 a.m. shortlist and a 9 p.m. “best of today” digest.
- Use extra alerts only for exceptional, short-lived deals.
- Use stable tags such as `#Under499`, `#Home`, `#Fashion`, `#Electronics` and `#PriceDrop`.
- Never claim “lowest ever,” scarcity or savings history without evidence.

## 30-day plan

| Period | Target | Execution |
|---|---:|---|
| Days 1–3 | 30–50 | Stabilize inventory; improve the pinned verification promise; enable reactions; create source-labeled invite links; prepare 24 hours of posts. |
| Days 4–7 | 100–150 | Invite a relevant opt-in founding audience; publish one original Reel/Short daily; participate only in communities that permit promotion; approach five small partners daily with a specific content swap. |
| Days 8–14 | 300–350 | Close five to eight micro-partnerships with college, hostel, budget-fashion, home and gadget communities; start subscriber deal submissions and a 20-person referral beta. |
| Days 15–21 | 550–650 | Double down on the best two categories, hooks and acquisition sources; create ten related short videos; run two partner “top five” roundups. |
| Days 22–30 | 1,000 stretch | Coordinate eight to twelve partners around one strong verified hero deal; publish daily original short video; repeat only sources with strong seven-day retention. |

If the channel is below 100 subscribers on day 10, the 1,000 target is unlikely without a new distribution partner or a paid experiment. If it reaches 300 by day 14 with good retention, the stretch target remains credible.

## Distribution loops

1. **Warm seed:** the owner personally shares the channel with relevant friends, colleagues and family. Do not add people without consent.
2. **Permission-based communities:** answer deal requests with evidence in groups that allow self-promotion or after moderator approval.
3. **Micro-partners:** exchange useful verified deal cards with adjacent public channels and small creators. Each partner gets a separate invite link.
4. **Original short video:** publish one 12–20 second Reel and YouTube Short per day with price proof, one reason the item is worth it, and the Telegram link in the profile.
5. **Deal Scouts:** accept public product links through channel DMs, verify them independently, and credit contributors only with consent.
6. **SEO:** publish useful pages such as “verified deals under ₹499,” merchant coupon explainers and honest expired-deal pages. Avoid thin pages that merely repeat merchant copy.
7. **Referral beta:** reward seven-day retained subscribers, not raw invites. Begin with recognition or early alerts; use monetary rewards only after revenue supports them.

## Measurement

Track daily:

- Net joins and leaves by invite source.
- Seven-day retained joins by source.
- One-hour and 24-hour views per subscriber.
- Forwards and reactions per view.
- Affiliate clicks, approved orders, confirmed commission and earnings per click.
- Queue coverage, stale rejections, price mismatches and failed sends.

Starting thresholds:

- 35%+ 24-hour view rate while below 500 subscribers.
- 3%+ affiliate click rate from post views.
- 2%+ forward rate on the strongest posts.
- 60%+ seven-day subscriber retention; require 70% before scaling a source.
- Below 2% price or availability error rate.
- Stop a source after 25 joins if seven-day retention is below 40%.

## Prohibited growth tactics

Do not buy followers, mass-DM strangers, auto-post into groups, copy competitor affiliate links, manufacture urgency, conceal affiliate relationships or run invite spam. These tactics create meaningless subscriber counts and put the Telegram and affiliate accounts at risk.
